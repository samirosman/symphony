# Design review: app shell, 2026-11-21

Reviewed against [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) using the *design-review* method:
screenshots of every workspace at fixed sizes, then a hierarchy, consistency,
states, feedback and accessibility check. Captured with
`scripts/screenshot.sh` at 1280×800 and 1024×640.

## Must fix
1. **Unchecked checkboxes and unselected radios were invisible** (Preferences:
   "Brush size cursor", "Symphony"). Their fill matched the window, which
   failed D4.4 (essential controls ≥ 3:1). **Fixed:** our own `checkbox` and
   `choice` widgets in `symphony-ui`, with a `line.control` edge (#80868F,
   3.9:1 on cards).
2. **Drawing names in Timeline exposure blocks were hidden** under the
   playhead and keyframe diamonds. **Fixed:** labels step past both.
3. **A stray scrollbar** showed under tab rows when tabs overflowed (1024 px).
   **Fixed:** tab-bar scrollbar hidden.

## Should fix
4. **Sliders**: hollow rectangular handles and a rail you could barely see.
   **Fixed:** filled rail up to the value, and round knobs.
5. **The Xsheet header ran past the card** in the narrow Hand-Drawn layout.
   **Fixed:** narrow headers keep only the transport and frame field.
6. **Preferences opened over the menu bar** with a collapse arrow. **Fixed:**
   centred, with no collapse arrow.

## Could improve (open)
- The Colour card repeats the selected swatch in its edit row. Name the
  swatch beside the grid and make editing a clear action.
- Cards with little content leave large empty areas (Colour, Tool
  Properties). Consider default workspace proportions closer to Harmony's.
- The Camera bottom toolbar mixes text buttons (Reset zoom, rotation, pan)
  with icon buttons. Give them icons and tooltips, or group them in a menu.
- The Preferences shortcut list is long. Group it by area, as Harmony's
  shortcut dialog does.

## What works well
- Card surfaces, gutters and the dark base read clearly. Tone separates
  areas without boxes.
- The amber frame tag and keyframe diamonds are the brightest things on
  the timeline, as intended.
- The stage reads as paper, and the Tools toolbar groups scan quickly.
