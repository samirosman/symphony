# AI in Symphony

Symphony is made for artists who draw their own work. This page says
exactly where AI is and isn't used, so you never have to guess.

## The short version
- **Symphony has no generative AI and no chatbots or language models (LLMs).**
  It never makes images, drawings, colours, poses, in-betweens or text for
  you, and it never will.
- **Right now Symphony uses no AI at all.**
- If we ever add an *assisted* tool, one that uses a trained model to
  help with a chore (for example suggesting which zones to fill with which
  colour), it:
  - runs entirely on your computer and never sends your work anywhere,
  - is never trained on your art, and never learns from it,
  - is **off until you turn it on**,
  - is clearly marked wherever it appears,
  - is listed on this page with what it does and how.
- One switch, **Preferences → AI → Use no AI**, turns off every assisted
  tool at once. It's on by default.
- Each scene records whether any assisted tool was ever used on it. If
  none were, the scene shows **No AI used**, and that record goes with the
  file, so you can show your work is entirely yours.

## What isn't AI
Many tools in an animation app are ordinary algorithms that follow fixed
rules, with no trained model involved. These are **not** AI, and they work
even with the switch on:
- Filling a closed area with paint (flood fill), closing small gaps.
- Smoothing and stabilising your strokes.
- Turning a scanned drawing into vector lines with a tracing algorithm.
- Interpolating keyframes, deformers, onion skin, effects and compositing.
- Lip-sync that analyses the sound wave with signal processing, if we
  build it that way. If a trained model is ever used instead, it becomes
  an assisted tool under the rules above.

## Why
Animators deserve tools that respect their craft and their ownership. Some
studios, festivals and clients ask artists to confirm they used no AI.
Symphony makes that easy to answer honestly.
