# Symphony 0.1.0 preview 1

The first preview for artist testers. Symphony is a free 2D animation app
built to feel familiar to Toon Boom Harmony users. This preview is early:
expect rough edges, and please tell us about them.

## What you can do
- **Draw** with Brush and Pencil: smooth, pressure-sensitive vector lines,
  with smoothing and a pressure response you can adjust.
- **Erase** with the Eraser tool or your pen's eraser end.
- **Animate**: draw on empty cells to create drawings, step frames with
  `,` and `.`, onion skin (Option/Alt+O), and change exposure with + and -.
- **Layers**: add, rename, hide and delete. The Timeline and Xsheet show
  the same timing.
- **Select and Transform**: move, scale, rotate and flip strokes, or whole
  layers with keyframes in Animate mode.
- **Move around the canvas**: Space to pan, Shift+Space to rotate, pinch
  to zoom.
- **Save and open** projects. Autosave and crash recovery are built in.
- **Export** an animated GIF or a PNG image sequence.
- **Shortcuts** follow Harmony 27's defaults.

## Not in this preview
Paint bucket, colour art layers, movie export, sound, deformers and
effects. See the plan on GitHub for everything that's coming.

## Installing
Use the Symphony Launcher. See
[Testing Symphony](https://github.com/tdemers218/symphony/blob/main/docs/TESTERS.md).
Builds aren't code-signed yet, so your computer warns you the first time.
The guide shows the one-time step.
