# Symphony 0.1.0 preview 2

A big step since the first preview. Symphony is a free 2D animation app
built to feel familiar to Toon Boom Harmony users. It's still early, so
please tell us about anything that feels rough.

## New since preview 1
- **Import anything**: one Import button in the top bar, or drag files
  onto the window. Pictures become layers, numbered pictures
  (walk_001, walk_002…) become a layer with a drawing per frame, sound
  lands on the Timeline with its waveform, and videos come in as frames
  plus their sound. Brushes, presets, projects and Harmony scenes import
  the same way.
- **Sound**: plays in step with your animation, and plays a short slice
  of sound for each frame as you step or scrub, for lip sync.
- **Textured brushes**, including Krita brush import.
- **Paint bucket**.
- **Node view**: a colour for each kind of node, easier connecting,
  Alt-drag to pull a node out of a chain (the chain heals), thumbnails
  when you hover a drawing node, and pegs that move their drawings live.
  Search for a blend mode such as "Multiply" to put it on the selected
  layer.
- **Search everything**: right-click any result to change its shortcut.
  If the new keys clash with another shortcut, Symphony asks you what to
  do until nothing clashes. Choose Harmony's shortcuts or Symphony's
  improved set in Preferences.
- **Drawing aids**: mirror view, light table, onion skin opacity and range,
  a playback range, and drag-to-reorder layers.
- **Windows**: dock, undock and resize views with snapping and animation.
  A new start screen shows your current frame behind it.
- **Faster**: smooth zooming on large screens, background rendering, and
  erasing heavy textured art is about 70 times faster.
- **Undo** keeps far more history, and the status bar says what was undone.
- **Lower-end computers**: turn off interface effects (blur, shadows,
  animation) in Preferences.
- **Crash reports**: if Symphony closes unexpectedly, it offers to send a
  report the next time it opens. You see the report before it's sent.

## Not in this preview yet
- Sound in exported movies.
- Moving or muting sound clips on the Timeline.
- iPhone videos in the default "High Efficiency" format. Set the iPhone
  camera to "Most Compatible", or convert the video to H.264 first.
- Video import and movie export need Cisco's free OpenH264 codec. Turn it
  on in Preferences > Video and Symphony downloads it from Cisco.

## Installing
Download the Symphony Launcher for your computer below, open it, and click
**Install Symphony**. Builds aren't signed with an Apple Developer ID, so
the first time you open the launcher, macOS asks you to allow it in
System Settings > Privacy & Security ("Open Anyway"). Windows may show
SmartScreen: click More info > Run anyway.
